What these show

  1. 01Collapses are declined by the affordability gate, not by the risk model.
  2. 02PD does not rise with the disaster, so the risk model is not a stress detector.
  3. 03A shock in the final three weeks is invisible to the forecast, which averages over a longer period.
  4. 04The anomaly lens always returns its cap, even in the calm scenario, so its list is a review queue and not an alarm.

Each scenario is a single business (N = 1). These are demonstrations, not estimates.
