THE MA'AL LOGO

One word, written twice in the same hand.

The Arabic logo is the master. The English wordmark is drawn from its parts, so both scripts carry the same weight, cuts and details. Colour: indigo #2E3192 only.

On every page load the letters rise into place and the madda lands last, in Arabic first and then in English.

What the English takes from the Arabic

01Heavy, even strokesEvery stroke has the same weight in both scripts, so the two words read as one family.
02Rising cutsThe tops of the tall strokes are cut on a slant that rises to the right, like the alef and lam.
03The meem blockThe solid block with a round counter is the meem. In English it becomes both a letters.
04The maddaThe small bar with a turned-down end sits over the alef in Arabic and is the apostrophe in English.
05The hooked tailThe lam ends in a hook with a sharp point. The English l ends the same way, turned to the right.
