Terms used on these pages

Glossary

Each term as this demo uses it, defined from the project's own documents. Tap or focus a dotted term on any page to see its definition there.

Baseline
The scenario with no extra disaster: the demo business exactly as configured. Every other scenario is compared with it.
Cash floor
A balance of zero. The forecast watches for a projected balance that falls below it.
Charity-directed late payment
Any late-payment penalty goes to charity and is never kept as income, because conventional late fees are not permitted.
Crunch alert
The forecast's warning level for a coming cash shortfall: none, watch or crunch.
CV AUC
How well the credit model ranks businesses that later default above those that do not, measured by cross-validation. Half is no better than chance; one is perfect.
Deductible liabilities
Payables and other short-term debts due within the Zakat year. They are subtracted from zakatable assets to give the Zakat base.
Flag reason
Why a transaction was placed in the review queue, given as a category such as an unusual amount or an unusual hour. It is not an accusation.
Hawl
One full lunar year of holding assets at or above the nisab. Zakat falls due only once the hawl is complete.
Insufficient data
Too little recent activity for any lens to run. It is a refusal to score, not a low result.
Markup fixed at signing
The markup is set once, when the contract is signed, and is never repriced during repayment.
Naive baseline
A simple forecast built from the trailing thirty-day average. The model has to beat it to earn its place.
Nisab
The minimum wealth on which Zakat is due, set by a weight of gold. A plate on the Zakat page shows whether the gold price behind it has been verified.
Oracle ceiling
The AUC of the generator's hidden risk index on this synthetic data: the most any model could reach here. It shows how much signal the data holds.
Output hash
A hash of the result itself, without its receipt. If a re-run gives the same output hash, the result was reproduced byte for byte.
Partial profile
The business has enough activity to be scored, but some features could not be computed. Each missing feature is listed with its reason.
PD (probability of default)
The credit model's estimate of how likely the business is to fall behind on repayment. It is a model output on synthetic data, not a measured rate.
Population (serving and research)
The generator makes two groups: a large research population for training and testing models, and a smaller serving population that the API and this demo show.
Receipt
The hashes and versions that produced a result: configuration, model, data, code and output. Anyone with the repository can re-run the result and compare.
Review queue
Transactions picked out for a person to look at. The lens fills a fixed number of slots for every business, so a full queue proves nothing by itself.
Rule table status
Whether the Sharia rule table has been confirmed. While it reads proposed and unverified, no advisor has certified it.
Scenario
The same demo business rebuilt in memory under a disaster picked from a fixed menu, so the disaster is the only difference.
Scope
The receipt's own statement of what it covers: the deterministic core only. It also names the command that verifies the result.
Sharia screen
A rule-based check of the business's activity against a rule table. It is pending confirmation by a Sharia advisor and is not a Sharia ruling.
Synthetic data
Data made by the project's generator from a fixed configuration. None of it comes from a real business.
Zakatable assets
Cash, inventory and receivables at the balance-sheet snapshot: the business assets Zakat is calculated on.

Synthetic data only. Population: serving. This demo holds no accounts, tracking cookies, analytics or logging of business data. A real deployment would need a PDPL notice, per-owner access and SAMA Open Banking consent.
