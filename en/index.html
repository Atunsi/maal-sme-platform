Why Ma'al

Most lenders hide the model or reduce it to a score. Ma'al shows its work.

01A receipt on every decisionEach result carries the hashes of its config, model, data and code. Re-run it with the same inputs and the output hash must match.
02Sharia-first, and honest about itThe screen is labelled as rule-based until a Sharia advisor confirms the rule table. Markup never changes after signing.
03Zakat you can followThe amount is built in front of you from the balance sheet, with a plate showing whether the gold price behind the nisab has been verified.
04Model numbers on the receiptThe credit model's cross-validated AUC and its ceiling are printed on every compliance view, straight from the receipt.

Questions

Is this a loan offer?OpenClose

No. Ma'al shows the output of a demo credit lens on synthetic data. It is not an offer from any bank, and the markup is not an APR.

Is the Sharia screen a religious ruling?OpenClose

No. It applies a rule table and says so on every page until a Sharia advisor confirms that table.

Is the data real?OpenClose

No. Every business in the demo is synthetic. A real deployment would need a PDPL notice, per-owner access and SAMA Open Banking consent.

What does a receipt let me do?OpenClose

It lists what produced the result, so anyone with the repository can re-run the decision and check that the output hash matches.
